# DjangoAir Baggage & In-Flight Options Policy

## Carry-On and Checked Luggage Allowance
Every DjangoAir ticket includes one cabin carry-on bag (up to 10kg, 55x40x20cm) and one personal item. Additional checked luggage (20kg) can be purchased during booking or at the airport counter through a Check-in Manager.

## Extra Luggage Fees at Airport Check-In
Check-in Managers may attach extra luggage options and assess overweight baggage fees at the airport counter prior to boarding pass issuance. All counter luggage fees are recorded on the ticket and itemized on the passenger's billing summary.

## Transfer of Paid Options During Involuntary Rebooking
When a passenger is rebooked onto an alternative flight due to an involuntary cancellation, paid meal options (Hot Lunch, Vegetarian Meal), Extra 20kg Luggage, and Priority Boarding remain eligible for transfer or equivalent Personal Cabinet credit.
