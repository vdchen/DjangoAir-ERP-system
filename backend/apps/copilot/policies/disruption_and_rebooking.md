# DjangoAir Flight Disruption, Cancellation & Rebooking Policy

## Involuntary Flight Cancellation Refund
When DjangoAir Operations cancels a scheduled flight, all paid bookings are automatically refunded 100% of the total paid amount directly to the passenger's Personal Cabinet balance. Passengers receive an email notice with their booking reference and refunded credit amount.

## Free Same-Route Involuntary Rebooking (72-Hour Waiver)
Passengers affected by an involuntary flight cancellation are entitled to complimentary rebooking onto the next available scheduled DjangoAir flight operating on the same origin-to-destination route within 72 hours, with a $0.00 change fee and full fare-difference waiver in the same cabin class.

## Voluntary Flight Change & Fare Difference
Passengers requesting a voluntary rebooking from an active (non-cancelled) scheduled flight may move to another scheduled flight on the same route subject to seat availability. Any positive fare difference is debited from the passenger's Personal Cabinet balance upon explicit passenger confirmation.

## Compensation & Care Vouchers (EU261 / DOT Standards)
For involuntary cancellations with less than 14 days notice on departures within the EU/UK or US DOT jurisdiction, passengers receive meal and refreshment vouchers for delays exceeding 2 hours, hotel accommodation for overnight disruptions, and statutory disruption assistance via Customer Care.

## Human-in-the-Loop Confirmation Requirement
No automated assistant or AI copilot may unilaterally modify bookings, cancel tickets, or debit customer funds. Every proposed rebooking must be presented clearly with flight number, departure time, cabin class, and fare difference, and requires explicit passenger or Supervisor confirmation before execution.
